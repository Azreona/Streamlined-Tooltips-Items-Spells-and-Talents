Take your WoW Classic Hardcore experience to the next level with Streamlined Tooltips, an addon designed to increase difficulty and deepen immersion. By stripping tooltips of detailed information, this addon forces you to rely on your instincts, experimentation, and game knowledge—just like a true adventurer.

Features:

Items:
Removes default descriptions and stat details.
Replaces rarity colors with unique textual flavor:
Poor (Gray): "This item feels utterly mundane."
Uncommon (Green): "This item feels slightly magical."
Legendary (Orange): "This item radiates legendary power."
Spells:
Erases all spell descriptions, leaving only the spell name. Experiment to learn their effects!
Talents:
Strips away talent descriptions, making every choice a mystery until unlocked.
Ultimate Challenge:
No more relying on tooltips to tell you everything. Make decisions like a true adventurer, where every item, spell, and talent is an opportunity to discover the unknown.
Why Use This Addon?

Enhanced Difficulty: Remove handholding and face the true challenge of WoW Classic Hardcore.
Increased Immersion: Engage with the world and your character by observing, testing, and learning without relying on detailed tooltips.
Satisfying Exploration: Rediscover the joy of figuring things out through trial, error, and intuition.
Compatibility:

Designed exclusively for WoW Classic Hardcore.
Works seamlessly with items, spells, and talents across all classes.
How to Use:
Install the addon and relive the adventure as it was meant to be—challenging, mysterious, and rewarding. No setup needed—just embrace the unknown!

Face the game like a true adventurer. With Streamlined Tooltips, knowledge must be earned, not handed out.
